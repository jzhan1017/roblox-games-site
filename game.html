The game

Smash The Piñata. It's a birthday party in the backyard. Hit the piñata, grab the candy, buy a bigger stick, and see how far you can get.

How you play

  1. Walk under the tree.
  2. Tap to swing.
  3. Candy falls out. Pick it up.
  4. The piñata bursts. A bigger one comes next.
  5. Spend your candy on a bigger stick.

What makes it fun

  • The piñata swings back. Hit it and it swings away on its rope.
  • Rope Uncle. He holds the rope and sometimes bonks you. The grass flashes red first, so you can step away.
  • A clock. The cake's candles burn down. Pop the piñata before they go out.
  • The King. Every 5th piñata wears a crown and rains golden candy.
  • No last level. It keeps going. Beat your best and you get on the leaderboard.

The shop

  • 18 sticks, from a wooden spoon up to a unicorn lance. You buy them with candy you earn.
  • Robux buys extra candy, a gold look, or candy rain for everyone in the game. Money never buys a stronger stick.

Made for kids

  • Learn it in 10 seconds.
  • Nothing to wait for longer than a minute.
  • Quiet sounds and a mute button, so a parent in the room doesn't mind.

The game is private for now, while it's being built.
